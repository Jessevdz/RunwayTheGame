Runway has no accounts. When you join a team or create a race, your browser is
handed a key and keeps it in its own storage. That key is the only thing that
says the phone belongs to your team, or that it is the host.

Anything that wipes the browser's storage throws the key away, and the server
cannot hand the same one back.

## What loses your place

| Cause | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Clearing browsing data, cookies, or site data | Wipes every key this browser holds |
| A private or incognito window | Forgets everything when the window closes |
| Switching browser or phone | The key stays behind on the old one |
| Opening the installed app instead of the browser you joined in | On iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own storage |

<Aside type="tip" title="Before you set off">
Join in the browser you will race in, outside a private window, and leave its
data alone until the race is over.
</Aside>

## A player lost their place

The team is fine. Its coins, power-ups, and progress live on the server, not on
the phone. The player only needs to join the team again.

**Before the race starts,** open the lobby again, enter your name, and tap your
squad. No code is needed.

**Once the race is live,** you need your team's code, and it comes from a
teammate who is still in:

<Steps>

1. On their race screen, the teammate scrolls down to **Your Squad** and taps **Invite**.

2. They tap **Copy Teammate Link** and send it to you. The link carries the team code.

3. You open the link itself, enter your name, and tap the squad. Pasting it into the box on the home screen does not work here; it drops the team code.

</Steps>

You are back on the team with everything it has earned. The old phone may still
be listed as a member; it does no harm.

<Aside type="caution" title="Nobody left to ask">
If every phone on a team has lost its place, or the player was racing alone,
the team cannot be recovered. The race still takes new squads, so you can start
a fresh one, but it begins from nothing.
</Aside>

## The host lost their place

Hosting belongs to the browser that created the race, and there is no way to
move it or get it back.

| When | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| Still in the lobby | Nobody can press start. Create a new race and share the new code |
| Race is live | Teams keep racing. Nobody can end the race early, settle a dispute, or use the host tools. The race ends by itself six hours after it was created |

If the host was also racing on a team, they can rejoin that team like any other
player. That gets them back into the race, not back into hosting.

## A solo run lost its place

A solo run is hosted and raced by the same phone, so it cannot be picked up
again. The run ends by itself after 24 hours and no time is posted. Start a new
run.

## After the race

An ended race cannot be joined, so a lost place cannot be reclaimed once the
race is over.
