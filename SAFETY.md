# Real-World Safety Guidelines

Runway is a real-world, location-based game that takes place in public spaces,
streets, and transit systems. **Your personal safety, the safety of others, and
respect for local laws always take precedence over the game.**

## 1. Golden Rule

**No waypoint, challenge, or coin reward is worth risking your physical safety.**
If completing a waypoint or challenge would require doing something unsafe,
illegal, or trespassing, **veto the challenge** or skip the waypoint. The veto
mechanic exists specifically so you can safely pass on any prompt.


## 2. Rules for Players

- **Look up from your screen.** Pay attention to traffic, obstacles, pedestrians,
  and moving vehicles at all times. Do not navigate or look at maps while
  crossing roads.
- **Obey all traffic and pedestrian laws.** Use designated pedestrian crossings,
  follow traffic signals, and never cross multi-lane highways or railway tracks
  outside authorized crossings.
- **Do not trespass.** Never enter private property, construction zones,
  fenced-off infrastructure, operational railway property, or restricted areas.
  If a waypoint is placed behind a locked gate or private barrier, do not attempt
  to reach it.
- **Respect bystanders.** Do not photograph uninvolved people, private
  residences, or minors. If someone asks not to be photographed, point your
  camera away immediately.
- **Nighttime and isolated areas.** Avoid playing alone in unlit, isolated, or
  unfamiliar areas after dark. Stick to well-lit public thoroughfares or play in
  pairs/groups.
- **Weather and physical limits.** Stay hydrated, dress appropriately for the
  weather, and do not push yourself beyond your physical limits.


## 3. Rules for Map Designers

When creating boards and routes in the Board Editor:

- **Place waypoints only in safe, publicly accessible locations.** Good locations
  include public plazas, parks, transit station concourses, public landmarks, and
  sidewalks.
- **Never place waypoints on:**
  - Active roadways or median strips.
  - Active railway tracks, yards, or rail infrastructure.
  - Private residential property or private corporate grounds without permission.
  - Dangerous terrain (cliffs, active construction, fast-moving waterways).
  - Restricted government or military facilities.
- **Design realistic challenge prompts.** Challenge prompts should never
  encourage reckless behaviour, trespassing, climbing unsafe structures, or
  disturbing the peace.

## 4. Rules for Hosts

- **Brief your players before the start.** Remind players about local traffic
  rules, boundary limits, and safety expectations.
- **Monitor the race.** If hazardous weather or unforeseen local conditions
  arise during a live event, use the host GM tools to pause or cancel the race.
- **Respect player disputes and safety vetoes.** Support players who prioritized
  safety over completing an awkward or hazardous waypoint.


## 5. Reporting an unsafe board

If you find a published board with a waypoint in a dangerous or private place, or
a challenge prompt that pushes players toward something unsafe or illegal, email
**[hello@playrunway.app](mailto:hello@playrunway.app)** with a link to the board
and which waypoint it is. Unsafe boards are taken down.

The same address handles a request to remove a photo you appear in; see
